## A) Option A modeling (correct handling of “local time”)
 
**Rule:** treat *local* birth datetime as **naive components** + **IANA timezone**, and treat **UTC** as the only absolute timestamp.
 
So the Birth Record stores:
 
 
- `birth_local_date` (`YYYY-MM-DD`)
 
- `birth_local_time` (`HH:MM[:SS]`)
 
- `birth_timezone` (IANA, e.g. `America/Los_Angeles`)
 
- `birth_utc_datetime` (`RFC3339 Z`)
 
- `julian_day` (float)
 

 
This avoids the category error of treating “local time” as an absolute instant.

# 5) One critical policy decision (DST ambiguity)
 
Two cases above create unavoidable ambiguity:
 
 
- fall-back “01:30 occurs twice”
 
- spring-forward “02:30 may not exist”
 

 
Your calculator must implement and record a policy in provenance/meta, e.g.:
 
 
- `tz_disambiguation_policy`: `"prefer_earliest"` or `"prefer_latest"`
 
- `tz_invalid_time_policy`: `"error"` or `"normalize_forward"`
 

 
**If you do not write this down, you will get silent, irreproducible drift later.**
